# AgRaz App Contents

AgRaz is a farm and family app. It helps you keep daily records of money, labour, milk, land, and papers, and also see market rates, weather, and government help.

Open any menu from the side menu (☰) or from Home.

Some menus need login. The main account can hide some menus for family members.

---

## Home

This is the first page after opening the app. You see farm pictures, short cuts to all menus, and Help.

---

## Main menus

### Income & Expense

This menu is for entering and keeping a record of all types of income and expenditure of daily farm and family life. Both farm money and house money can be written here, so you know where money came from and where it went.

First choose **Income** or **Expense**. Then choose a **category**, then a **sub category**. You also enter the amount, date, person’s name, village and address if needed, and how money was paid (cash or transfer). You can link the entry to a farm / organization if you have more than one.

**Narration** is a short note in your own words — for example “areca sold to trader”, “bought urea”, or “school fees”. It explains that one entry.

**Income categories and sub categories**

- **Farming Income** — Agriculture Production, By-products, Livestock & Dairy, Rental / Service
- **Non-Farming Income** — Government / Subsidy, Asset & Miscellaneous

**Expense categories and sub categories**

- **Farming Expense** — Labour, Manure, Chemicals, Machinery Rent, Vehicle Rent, Implements, Irrigation, Machinery Purchase, Special Works, Cattle Feed, Fodder, Vet Medicines and Care, Live Stock Purchase, Others
- **Living Expense** — Grocery, Fruits & Veg, Milk & Ghee, Medicine, Vehicle Maintenance, Pooja, Donation, Gift, Mobile & Currency, Lifestyle, Misc, Vehicle Rent, Refreshing, Gas, Education, Electricity, Repair, Transportation Expense, Entertainment, Tour & Travel, Newspapers & Books, Lifestyle Others

**What you can see later**

- **View All** — full list of every entry. You can search by type (Income / Expense), category, sub category, and date.
- **Reports**
  - **Overview** — total income, total expense, and net (what is left). Also month vs all time, how this month compares with last months, and which categories bring most income or take most expense.
  - **Monthly** — income, expense, and net for each month.
  - **Weekly** — same picture week by week.
  - **Category** — how much went to each category and each sub category (chart and list). You can open a category to see its sub categories.

### Manage Organization

This menu is for keeping separate books when you have more than one farm, group, or unit. Each organization is like one farm name. Under it you keep **ledgers** (simple account heads, like cash book names) and write income or expense against a ledger.

You enter amount, date, narration, cash or transfer. If you choose transfer, money can move from one organization to another. Narration is the reason — for example “areca sale” or “fertilizer purchase for this farm”.

**What you can see later**

- List of entries for the selected farm and ledger (income as credit, expense as debit).
- **Organization reports**
  - **Org-wise** — income, expense, and balance of each farm / unit.
  - **Ledger-wise** — balance of each ledger.
  - **Monthly** — month by month for the selected farm or ledger.
  - **Weekly** — week by week.

### Labour Management

This menu is for keeping a full record of farm labour — who worked, what work, what rate, what you owe, and what you already paid. It is the worker’s account book.

You can enter work as **Daily Wages** or **Contract**. For contract you also write the labour head (the person who brought the team). For each person you write name, category of work, shift, days/hours, gender, and rate. Extra costs like rent, food, and bonus can also be added. Payment is entered separately when you give money. Mark Tally is used when you close / agree the account with a worker. Narration is optional — for example “plucking in block 2” or “advance paid”.

**Work categories** — Plucking, Cutting, Drying, Grading, Packing, Transport, Others, Opening Balance. You can also add your own category.

**Shifts** — full day, morning, evening, night.

**What you can see later**

- **Summary** — each worker’s name, total payable, total paid, and balance still due (or extra paid).
- Work broken by **category** and by **shift**.
- **History** — all work and payment lines, with date, category, location, narration.
- You can take an **Excel** file or a **PDF statement** for a worker (like a labour bill / account copy).

---

### Labour Work Entry

This menu is for writing the work done on a day, so the family can see and confirm it. Use this when more than one person looks after labour on the farm.

You enter worker name, work category (same as above: Plucking, Cutting, Drying, and so on), shift, days/hours, rate, gender, and narration. There is also a **Receipt** part when money is received against that work, and a **Confirm** part where family members accept or reject a shared entry.

**What you can see later**

- **History / Reports** — total receivable, total received, and balance, plus the list of work entries by name and date.
- Pending work waiting for family confirmation is also shown on Home.

### Dairy

This menu is for the **farmer** who gives milk to a dairy or buys milk. It keeps the daily milk and payment record with that dairy / person.

You choose the type of entry:

- Milk given
- Milk bought
- Payment received
- Payment made

Then you enter dairy / party name, mobile, shift (morning or evening), quantity in liters, rate per liter, amount, date, and narration (for example “evening milk” or “advance”).

**What you can see later**

- **Account** summary — total liters given, liters bought, money receivable, money payable, and net (who owes whom).
- Full list of entries with date, type, liters or amount, and narration.

---

### Dairy Owner

This menu is for the **dairy owner** who collects milk from farmers and also sells milk. It is the dairy’s customer book.

You keep a **customer list** (name, mobile, village, default rate). Then you enter:

- Milk collected
- Milk sold
- Paid to customer
- Received from customer

Same extra details as Dairy: shift, liters, rate, amount, narration.

**What you can see later**

- Summary of liters collected / sold and money payable / receivable.
- Customer list and all entries against each customer.

---

### Notes

This menu is for daily farm and house notes that are not money accounts. Things you should not forget — a thought, a reminder, or a list.

You can make two kinds:

- **Note** — a short write-up.
- **List** — a checklist (you tick items when done).

You can put a **label** on a note (like money, work, food, home, calendar) so similar notes stay together. You can keep a list of common list items and reuse them.

**What you can see later**

- **History** — all old notes and lists, so you can open, change, or delete them.

---

### Future Plans

This menu is for writing the work you want to do later, with a rough cost, so you can arrange money, people, and material before the season.

You give the plan a name, a date, and one or more lines of work. Each line has a description and an **estimate cost** in rupees. Later you can change status:

- Planned
- In progress
- Completed
- Cancelled

You can also write actual cost and end date when the work is finished.

**What you can see later**

- **Plans history** — all plans by year/month, total estimate, and status. Open a plan to update it.

---

### Market Reports

This menu is not for entering your own sales. It is for **seeing market prices** so you know what APMC / market is paying today, and you can choose a better time and place to sell.

You can look by date, APMC, taluk, and crop variety. You see arrival quantity, min price, max price, and average price. You also see how average price moved over recent days.

**What you can see**

- Today’s rates list (variety, min, max, average).
- How much stock arrived in the market.
- Price trend chart for selected varieties.
- Comparison across markets / taluks.

Needs internet.

---

### Weather Report

This menu is for knowing the weather of your area so you can plan spraying, harvest, drying, and field work.

You see today’s temperature, rain, rain chance, and wind, a **7-day forecast**, and short **farm advice** for the coming week.

Needs internet.

---

### General Services

This menu is for finding people who do farm and village work, or for putting your own service so other farmers can call you.

You browse by **main category** and **sub category**, search by name, and see contact details.

**Main categories (with examples of sub categories)**

- **Farming Services** — labour supply, harvesting, spraying, drone, tractor, JCB, manure, irrigation, peeling, areca sorting, coconut harvesting, and many similar farm jobs
- **Animal Husbandry** — feed, fodder, medicine, milking machine, doctors, inseminators
- **Passenger Vehicle** — car, jeep, tempo, bus, bike, rickshaw
- **Goods Vehicle** — pick up, tractor, lorry, and similar
- **JCB / Hitachi**
- **Medicine**
- **Other Services** — electrician, plumber, painter, welder, solar, building work, and similar village services

When you register your service you write business name, category, sub category, contact, and remarks (what you do).

---

### Buy & Sell

This menu is a small **market place** inside the app. You can see what others are selling, or list your own farm goods.

On **Browse** you see item name, photo, price, and category. On **My Items** you add your own item (title, price, category, photo, details) and manage what you listed.

Use this for seeds, fertilizer, harvested crop, or other farm things you want to buy or sell.

---

### Farmer Education

This menu is for learning better farming. Right now the guide is mainly **arecanut farming**.

You can read simple topics under:

- Nursery & Planting
- Processing Methods
- Pest & Disease
- Harvesting

There can be text and video. Language can be English or Kannada.

This is reading / learning only. You do not enter farm records here.

---

### Government Facilities

This menu is for knowing **government help** for farmers — not for applying from inside the app, but for seeing what schemes exist.

You first choose department, then crop, then type of help. Types include:

- Loans
- Insurance
- Grants

Then you see the list of schemes with name, short details, department, crop, and a link if more information is given.

Needs internet.

---

### RTC Entry

This menu is for keeping your **land RTC** (land record) on the phone, like a copy of the revenue paper.

You enter district, taluk, hobli, survey number, hissa, and land size in acre, gunta, and ana. You can write extra details (owner self, joint holders, cultivated, arecanut, paddy, well, and similar notes) and attach a photo / file of the land paper.

**What you can see later**

- Full list of all land pieces you saved, with survey number, area, and notes. You can open one to change it.

---

### Documents

This menu is for keeping **family papers** as photos, so Aadhaar, PAN, and similar documents are not only on paper at home.

You create a **folder** for each family member (folder name = person’s name). Inside you add a document name and one or more photos. You can also keep a document without a folder.

**What you can see later**

- Folders and documents list. Open a name to see the photos. You can rename or delete a folder or a paper.

---

### Event Manage

This menu is for remembering important dates of family and farm life — birthday, insurance renewal, loan due, festival, or any other reminder.

You enter event name, date, and time for the alarm. You can repeat it yearly, monthly, weekly, or daily. The phone can ring at that time.

**What you can see later**

- List of all saved events with next date and how often they repeat. You can change or delete any reminder.

---

### Feedback

This menu is for telling the AgRaz team what is useful and what is a problem.

You write a message in your own words and can mention which menu it is about. If you are logged in, you can also see your earlier messages.

This does not keep farm records. It only helps improve the app.

---

## Account

### Profile

This is your personal page. You see and update your name and phone number, so your account details stay correct.

### Family members

This is only for the **main account**. Here you add family people with their own email and password. Their labour, dairy, and expense entries save into the same farm account. You can turn menus on or off for each person — for example one person may use Dairy but not Income & Expense.

### Settings

Here you choose app language (**English** or **Kannada**), light / dark look, password change or reset, and whether the phone should show reminders. You can also sign out.

### About Team

Here you can read who made AgRaz.

### Login / Logout

Login is to open your saved records. Logout is to close your account on that phone.

---

## Help Center (from Home)

- **Getting Started** — simple steps to begin using the app.
- **FAQ** — common questions (account, language, dairy, labour, documents, and similar).
- **Contact Support** — reach the AgRaz team.
- **Send Feedback** — same as the Feedback menu.

---

**Note:** Market rates, weather, and government schemes need internet. Money, labour, dairy, notes, and similar records can also be kept when the phone is offline, and they go to your account when internet comes back.
